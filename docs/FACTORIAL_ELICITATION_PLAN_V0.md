## Scope is not operator commutativity

The visible 2×2 form grid shows that two independently *visible markers* can
co-occur. It does **not** prove the same meaning for `Q(LOOP(p))` and
`LOOP(Q(p))`. The former could ask about a repeated state; the latter would
be a repeated act of questioning, which may be unnatural or prohibited.
Only an author-supplied contrast can decide.

## Mathematical acoustic link (hypothesis only)

Two binary symbolic distinctions require four distinct abstract states. A
two-bit idealized label is one possible representation; assigning bits to
phase, fundamental frequency, harmonic intervals or temporal gaps is an
**engineering codebook choice**, not a discovery about Ech’ta’reen speech.
Any proposed sound renderer needs separate tests for audible discriminability,
timing/noise tolerance, out-of-code abstention, and the true-name E26 prohibition.
