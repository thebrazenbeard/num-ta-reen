Changelog
v2.0 (2025-12-31): Clean-pass rebuild of Dictionary v2.0 content to ensure strict compliance with the reference grammar’s phoneme inventory and morphology; replaces earlier draft blocks containing non-inventory consonants and contact-transparent artifacts.
Method & assumptions
This is a xenolinguist-grade analytical lexicon. Native entries obey the grammar’s constraints: - ’ = /ʔ/ phonemic boundary stop. - No tense; echo-status and evidentiality are grammaticalized. - Baseline lexicon avoids ship metaphors and transparent Common roots. - Anchor creole/contact items are permitted only when tagged {AC} (CONTACT/DEPRECATED).
Entry format
NNN. headword /IPA/ [CAT] {REG} (TABOO) — gloss. Der: related forms. Ex: E##.
CAT: STATE, ACT, ENTITY, ECHO, PART, CLITIC, DERIV
REG: CF, FC, RT, AC
TABOO: NONE, RT-ONLY, CLP-ADJ, TRUE-NAME, DEAD-NAME, CONTACT, DEPRECATED
Abbreviations key (IGT cross-reference)
AGT agent; PAT patient; SRC source; GOAL goal; INST instrument; CONS consent/permission; EVID evidential; WIT witnessed; VRF verified; LOOP loop/recurrence; RET return-pattern; CLP collapse adjacency; INV inverse/misaligned; TOP topic; FOC focus; Q interrogative; COMP complementizer; REL relativizer; NEG negation particle; PROH prohibition/alarm.
